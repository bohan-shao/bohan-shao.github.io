I'm Bohan Shao 邵渤涵, an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg). I received my B.S. in Computer Science from [Harbin Institute of Technology](http://www.hit.edu.cn), where my research focused on Multimodal AI, advised by Professor Hongli Zhang.

Previously, I worked as an AI Agent Development Intern at DP Technology, where I built core AI capabilities for [SciMaster](https://scimaster.bohrium.com) — an intelligent platform for academic research covering academic Q&A, deep research, paper search, literature review generation, and academic writing, enabling end-to-end intelligent workflows for researchers.
